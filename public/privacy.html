Expense Manager

Expense Manager is a private, single-user web app for recording personal expenses and income, organising them by account, category and payment method, and viewing totals and balances. It is used only by its owner, behind a password; there are no other user accounts and no public sign-up.

Open the app (password required).

Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Data the app stores

The app stores the financial records its owner enters (transactions, accounts, categories, payees, tags, attachments and a change history) in the owner's own Cloudflare D1 database. This data is not sold, shared with or disclosed to anyone.

Google user data

The owner can optionally connect Google Drive to keep a backup copy of their data. When connected, the app requests:

  • Google Drive (only files this app creates) — used solely to create and update one backup file, expense-manager-backup.json, and to read it back if the owner chooses to restore. The app cannot see or change any other files in Drive.
  • Email address — used only to check that the Google account connecting is the owner's own account. Any other account is rejected and its access is revoked immediately.

A Google refresh token is stored encrypted, so daily automatic backups can run. Google user data is used only to provide this backup feature. It is not used for advertising, not transferred or sold to anyone, and not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Google Drive at any time in the app (Manage → Data → Disconnect), which revokes the app's access and deletes the stored token. Access can also be removed from the Google Account's Third-party connections page. Backup files already saved in Drive stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them.
